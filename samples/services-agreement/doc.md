# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into by Acme Corp. ("Customer") and Widget LLC ("Provider").

## RECITALS

Customer wishes to engage Provider to perform the Services.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 **Defined Terms.** In this Agreement:

(a) "**Affiliate**" means any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party;

(b) "**Services**" means the services described in Section 2.1; and

(c) "**Fees**" means the amounts set out in Schedule 1.

1.2 **Interpretation.** Headings are for convenience only.

## 2. Services

2.1 **Performance.** Provider shall perform the Services:

(a) in a professional manner;

(b) in accordance with applicable law, including:

(i) data protection law; and

(ii) export control law; and

(c) on the timeline agreed by the parties.

2.2 **Personnel.** Provider shall assign qualified personnel. Provider may subcontract with Customer's consent.

This sentence continues Section 2.2 as an unnumbered paragraph.

## 3. Fees

3.1 **Payment.** Customer shall pay the Fees within 30 days of invoice.

3.2 **Late Payment.** Late amounts bear interest at 1% per month, provided that:

(a) interest shall not exceed the maximum rate permitted by law; and

(b) no interest accrues on amounts disputed under Section 3.3.

Invoices are delivered:

• by email; or

• by courier.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement continues until terminated under Section 4.2.

4.2 Either party may terminate on 30 days' notice, in which case:

(a) Customer pays for Services performed;

(b) Provider returns Customer materials.
